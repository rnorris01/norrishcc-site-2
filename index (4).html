Case Framing Approach

Each case is evaluated independently, considering the information available at the time, applicable regulatory requirements, accepted professional standards, institutional responsibilities, and the hospital's operational circumstances.

Dr. Norris examines whether appropriate patient safety safeguards, policies, staffing practices, communication pathways, supervisory processes, and escalation procedures were established, implemented, and followed.

How Cases Are Evaluated

Dr. Norris conducts a systematic review of the available evidence, including relevant medical records, hospital policies and procedures, staffing documentation, incident reports, witness testimony, and applicable regulatory and accreditation standards.

His analysis considers the hospital's operational environment, the information reasonably available to decision-makers, and whether institutional safeguards and escalation processes were appropriate and effectively implemented.

The objective is to determine whether the hospital fulfilled its administrative, operational, and patient safety responsibilities based on the facts and circumstances of the case.
