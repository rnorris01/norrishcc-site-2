About Dr. Norris

Dr. Norris is a hospital administration expert witness with more than 25 years of healthcare experience and 15 years in executive leadership across academic medical centers, inpatient rehabilitation hospitals, long-term acute care hospitals, behavioral health, and community hospitals.

His work focuses on evaluating whether hospital operations and leadership decisions were reasonable under the circumstances and consistent with accepted standards of care.
