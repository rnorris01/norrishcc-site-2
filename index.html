About Dr. Norris

Dr. Ron Norris is a hospital administration expert witness with more than 25 years of healthcare experience, including 15 years in executive leadership roles across academic medical centers, behavioral health, inpatient rehabilitation hospitals, long-term acute care hospitals, and community hospital settings.

His work focuses on evaluating whether hospital operations, leadership decisions, and system performance met accepted standards of care, regulatory requirements, and what a reasonable hospital would have done under similar circumstances.

Dr. Norris has been retained in cases involving hospital negligence, wrongful death, patient safety events, and regulatory compliance, providing clear, defensible opinions grounded in how hospitals actually function in real-world environments.

His analyses integrate CMS Conditions of Participation, Joint Commission standards, and operational best practices, with a focus on system failures, escalation processes, and administrative decision-making that impact patient outcomes.
